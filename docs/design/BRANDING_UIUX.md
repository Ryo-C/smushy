# **Smushy – Branding & UI/UX Reference Guide**

> For core philosophy and values, see [VALUES.md](VALUES.md)

This guide focuses on visual design elements and UI/UX implementation.

---

## **1. Brand Overview**

* **Name:** Smushy
* **Type:** Fun, playful, affectionate, adult-friendly, modern
* **Core Idea:** Strengthen bonds between couples and families by reducing friction points, encouraging shared experiences, and fostering emotional closeness.
* **Target Users:** Couples, families, close loved ones looking for connection, organization, and playful bonding.

---

## **3. Taglines & Messaging**

* **Primary Tagline:** “Moments that bring us closer.”

* **Elevator Pitch:**

  > “Smushy is the app that helps couples and families stay connected, organized, and emotionally close. From shared calendars and to-do lists to playful reminders, Smushy makes life together easier, fun, and full of meaningful moments.”

---

## **4. Logo & Icon**

* **Concept:**

  * Two abstract figures leaning together to symbolize closeness, partnership, and bonding.
  * Rounded shapes for warmth and approachability.
* **Typography:**

  * Rounded, sans-serif fonts (e.g., Poppins, Nunito, Quicksand)
  * Soft and friendly letters, avoid sharp edges.
* **App Icon:**

  * Abstract figures on a warm pastel background.
  * Rounded corners, minimalistic, instantly recognizable at small sizes.

---

## **5. Color Palette**

Smushy's color palette is defined in the source code theme system.

**Source of Truth:**
* Theme definitions: `src/shared/theme/presets.ts`
* Tailwind configuration: `tailwind.config.js`

**Current themes:**
* **Warm (Default):** Coral/Cream/Sage - nurturing, playful, emotionally safe
* **Serene:** Teal/Peach - calm, balanced, still warm

**Design Principles:**
* All colors maintain warmth (no stark whites or cold blues)
* WCAG AA accessibility minimum (4.5:1 contrast for text)
* Soft edges, rounded corners, gentle gradients
* Emotional safety through visual comfort

**Color Philosophy:**
* Coral provides warmth without aggression
* Cream adds comfort and approachability
* Sage brings calm balance and success states
* All colors work together to create a safe, inviting atmosphere

## **6. Feature Branding & Microcopy**

**Core Features** (aligned with [VALUES.md](VALUES.md)):

* **Moments** → Shared couple-specific events (dates, trips, rituals, milestones)
  * Syncs to device calendar
  * Emotion-forward, not logistical

* **Our Story** → Memory feed timeline
  * Shared timeline of Smushy-created Moments
  * Photos, notes, emojis, milestones
  * Narrative, not archival

* **Check-ins** → Guided reflection prompts
  * Optional, gentle, private by default
  * Share only with consent
  * Low-pressure emotional check-ins

* **Rituals** → Recurring couple rituals
  * Weekly date night, monthly reflection
  * Soft reminders, not streaks
  * Warm and intentional

* **Nudges** → Gentle reminders
  * Soft prompts to create or revisit Moments
  * Anniversary reminders
  * Non-judgmental, suggestive

**Reminders / Notifications:** Warm, affectionate tone:
* "Time for a little Smushy moment ❤️"
* "Remember what matters today"
* "Your weekly ritual is coming up"

**Note:** Smushy intentionally avoids chat/messaging, effort tracking, and productivity features. See [VALUES.md](VALUES.md) for philosophy.

---

## **7. UI/UX Principles**

* **Overall Feel:** Cozy, friendly, light, modern.
* **Micro-interactions:**

  * Soft bounces, playful animations when tasks are completed or reminders pop up.
  * Hugs or tandem motifs for interaction feedback.
* **Navigation:**

  * Clear separation between **Couple Mode** and **Family Mode** (tabs or dashboard sections).
  * Smooth transitions, consistent visual language across modes.
* **Onboarding:**

  * Introduce Smushy as a companion for connection and bonding.
  * Emphasize the fun and playful nature of the app.

---

## **8. Brand Experience**

* **Voice in App:** Friendly, encouraging, lighthearted.
* **Scalable Identity:**

  * Core “Smushy moments” concept applies to couples and families.
  * Fun, affectionate moments across life stages: dating → living together → family → extended family.
* **Emotional Hook:** Reinforces daily, small acts of closeness, making the app part of users’ emotional routine.

---

## **9. Visual & Illustration Guidelines**

* Rounded, soft shapes and icons
* Minimalistic, abstract human figures for universality
* Soft pastel gradients for warmth and friendliness
* Playful but not childish — keep mature and modern tone
* Illustrations can depict:

  * Paired activity
  * Shared household tasks
  * Playful interactions / bonding moments

---

### **10. Optional Naming & Variations for Scaling**

* **Brand Extensions / Unique Handles:**

  * Smushy.io → playful tech feel
  * Smush.ly → cute, modern
  * Smushie → slightly softer, flexible
  * SmushyLife → emphasizes family & lifestyle

---

### ✅ **Summary**

Smushy is designed to be:

* **Fun, modern, and warm**
* **Flexible** for both couples and families
* **Emotionally resonant**, giving users a reason to open the app daily
* **Visually distinctive** and easily brandable
* **Lighthearted but adult-friendly**, not childish
