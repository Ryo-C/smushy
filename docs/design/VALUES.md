# Smushy — Core Values & Features

> A relationship companion app for couples who want to be more intentional, emotionally connected, and thoughtful with each other.

---

## Who Is Smushy For?

### ✅ Smushy is FOR:

- **Couples who communicate reasonably well** and are open to reflection
- **Long-term or married couples**
- **Busy professionals** who forget small but meaningful moments
- **Long-distance couples**
- **Couples navigating life transitions** (moving, career changes, children)

#### These users believe:

- Love is built through small, intentional acts
- Remembering matters
- Emotional connection requires care, not constant conversation
- Not every feeling needs to be debated — some just need to be noticed

---

### 🚫 Smushy is NOT FOR:

- Couples in active, high-conflict relationships
- Partners looking to prove who's right
- People seeking therapy, arbitration, or validation
- Users who want to track effort, blame, or emotional imbalance

> **Smushy is deliberately designed to discourage misuse by design.**

---

## What Does Smushy Do?

### 1. Shared Couple Moments

- Create and manage couple-specific events (dates, trips, rituals, milestones)
- These events sync to the device's iOS or Google calendar
- Personal calendars remain private and untouched
- **Smushy handles intention. The OS calendar handles logistics.**

### 2. Memory Feed

- A private timeline of shared moments created in Smushy
- Photos, notes, emojis, and milestones
- Focused on positive, intentional experiences
- No scraping of personal calendar data
- **This becomes the couple's shared memory space, not a chat log**

### 3. Gentle Emotional Reflection

- Optional, private reflection prompts
- First-person, non-blaming language
- Share only when both partners are comfortable
- No comments, no replies, no arguments
- **Reflection over reaction**

### 4. Rituals & Reminders

- Recurring couple rituals (weekly date night, monthly check-in)
- Soft reminders to stay connected
- Celebrations of anniversaries and milestones
- **No pressure. No streaks. No guilt.**

---

## What Smushy Intentionally Avoids

- ❌ No chat or messaging
- ❌ No shared emotional rant logs
- ❌ No effort counters or streaks
- ❌ No conflict resolution tools
- ❌ No reading or analyzing personal calendars
- ❌ No forced emotional visibility

> **Smushy is not a scoreboard, a judge, or a therapist.**

---

## Core Philosophy

**Smushy exists to make good relationships stronger, not to fix broken ones.**

It's built around:

- 🛡️ Emotional safety
- 🤝 Consent
- 🎯 Intentionality
- 💛 Warmth
- 🔒 Respect for privacy

## Core Features & Naming

### 1. Moments
**Feature:** Shared Couple Events

#### What it is
- Couple-specific events created in Smushy
- Dates, trips, rituals, anniversaries, meaningful plans
- Synced to iOS / Google calendar
- Editable only inside Smushy

#### Why "Moments"?
- Emotion-forward, not logistical
- Doesn't feel like a task or meeting
- Encourages intention ("This matters")

#### Avoid
- "Events" (too clinical)
- "Tasks" or "Plans" (productivity vibes)

---

### 2. Our Story
**Feature:** Memory Feed

#### What it is
- A shared timeline of Smushy-created Moments
- Photos, notes, emojis, milestones
- Highlight past memories gently

#### Why "Our Story"?
- Emphasizes continuity and shared history
- Feels narrative, not archival
- Encourages reflection without judgment

#### Avoid
- "History"
- "Timeline"
- "Log"

---

### 3. Check-ins
**Feature:** Guided Reflection

#### What it is
- Optional, gentle reflection prompts
- Private by default
- Share only with consent
- No replies, no debates

#### Why "Check-ins"?
- Low-pressure
- Familiar and emotionally safe
- Doesn't imply conflict

#### Avoid
- "Journals"
- "Feedback"
- "Feelings"
- "Reviews"

---

### 4. Rituals
**Feature:** Recurring Couple Rituals

#### What it is
- Recurring Moments (weekly date night, monthly reflection)
- Soft reminders
- Visible in Our Story

#### Why "Rituals"?
- Warm and intentional
- Emotionally meaningful
- Not performance-driven

#### Avoid
- "Habits"
- "Streaks"
- "Challenges"

---

### 5. Nudges
**Feature:** Gentle Reminders

#### What it is
- Soft prompts to create or revisit Moments
- Anniversary reminders
- Reflection suggestions

#### Why "Nudges"?
- Non-judgmental
- Suggestive, not demanding

#### Avoid
- "Alerts"
- "Warnings"
- "To-dos"

---

### 6. Notes to Self
**Feature:** Personal Reflections (Private Only)

#### What it is
- Private reflections tied to Moments
- Not shared unless explicitly chosen
- Can fade or archive over time

#### Why this works
- Encourages introspection without exposure
- Prevents venting becoming permanent

---

### 7. Calendar Sync
**Feature:** Calendar Sync (Supporting Feature)

#### What it is
- Smushy Moments appear in iOS / Google calendar
- Smushy only reads/writes its own Moments
- No personal calendar access

#### Why keep the name simple
- This is infrastructure, not the product
- Avoid over-promising
